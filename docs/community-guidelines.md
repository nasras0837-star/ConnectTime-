# Connect Community Guidelines

## Freedom of expression

Connect supports freedom of expression and lively, good-faith discussion. Members may share opinions, criticism, personal experiences, humor, art, political and social viewpoints, and unpopular ideas, provided their expression follows the law and these guidelines. Disagreement, criticism, satire, or a viewpoint someone dislikes is not harassment by itself.

## Harassment and intimidation are prohibited

Do not target, threaten, bully, intimidate, stalk, or repeatedly contact someone who has asked you to stop. Do not encourage others to pile on, threaten violence, expose private information, blackmail, or use sexualized abuse to frighten or silence someone. Credible threats and encouragement or coordination of real-world harm are prohibited.

Challenge claims, arguments, public actions, and ideas without targeting a person with abuse. Slurs or dehumanizing attacks targeting people based on protected or personal characteristics are not allowed. Impersonation intended to deceive or humiliate is prohibited.

## Privacy, consent, and safety

Do not publish another person's private contact details, home address, financial or identity information, private messages, or intimate imagery without permission. Non-consensual intimate content, sexual exploitation, and any sexual content involving minors are prohibited. Connect is an adults-only 18+ community.

## Moderation principles

Reports should be reviewed in context, distinguishing disagreement and criticism from targeted abuse. Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include no action, content removal, a warning, temporary restrictions, or account suspension. Credible threats, doxxing, stalking, or severe abuse may require immediate action. Members should be told the reason for significant enforcement and have a way to appeal where appropriate.

## Product readiness

These guidelines define the intended standard. In-app reporting, blocking, moderation review, and appeals must be implemented and tested before the community opens fully. Do not represent these controls as available until they are operational.
