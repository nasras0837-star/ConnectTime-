# Connect Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy

**Operator:** Techcyber LLC  
**Service:** Connect

Connect respects copyright and other intellectual property rights. Members may upload, post, transmit, stream, or otherwise make available only material that they created, own, or are authorized to use. Access to content on the internet does not itself grant permission to copy, repost, download, distribute, publicly perform, or commercially exploit it.

## Prohibited conduct

Members must not:
- Upload or share copyrighted photos, videos, music, articles, broadcasts, artwork, software, or other material without permission or another valid legal basis.
- Remove or alter copyright notices, watermarks, attribution, or rights-management information to conceal the source or ownership of content.
- Circumvent technical access controls or redistribute content in violation of applicable terms or law.
- Impersonate a rights holder or submit knowingly false copyright complaints.

## Member responsibility

By submitting content, a member represents that they own it or have all permissions, licenses, releases, and other rights needed for the specific use on Connect. Members should retain records of licenses and permissions. A member's responsibility includes music, background audio, photographs, video clips, livestreams, reposts, and material supplied by third parties.

A member must promptly remove content if they discover that they lack the necessary rights. Connect may remove or disable access to content, restrict features, suspend accounts, or terminate repeat infringers, subject to applicable law and review.

## Reporting infringement

A copyright owner or authorized agent may submit a notice through the [Connect Copyright Protection page](/copyright). Include:
1. A physical or electronic signature of the owner or authorized agent.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (or a representative list where appropriate).
3. Identification of the specific material claimed to be infringing and information reasonably sufficient for Connect to locate it.
4. Contact information, such as a mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
5. A statement of good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement that the notice information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that the submitter is the owner or authorized to act for the owner of an exclusive right allegedly infringed.

Connect may request missing information before treating a notice as complete. The online form is an intake channel and does not replace any statutory requirements or guarantee a particular outcome.

## Counter-notifications

If material is removed or disabled because of a copyright notice, the affected member may submit a counter-notification where permitted by law. A valid counter-notification generally requires the member's signature, identification of the removed material and its prior location, a statement under penalty of perjury of a good-faith belief that removal resulted from mistake or misidentification, and the required consent-to-jurisdiction and service-of-process statements. Connect will handle valid counter-notifications and any restoration in accordance with applicable law.

## Repeat infringers and enforcement

Connect may, in appropriate circumstances and at its discretion, terminate accounts of users who are repeat infringers. Connect may also remove or disable access to reported material, limit posting or upload privileges, preserve relevant records where legally appropriate, and take other proportionate action. Serious or repeated violations may result in permanent account termination.

## No guarantee of automated detection

Connect may use reports, human review, and technical controls to address infringement. No automated system can reliably identify every copyrighted work or determine every fair use, license, or other legal exception. Members and rights holders should use the reporting process for specific concerns.

## Designated copyright agent

Before Connect publicly launches a U.S. DMCA safe-harbor program, Techcyber LLC must designate and register a DMCA agent with the U.S. Copyright Office and publish the agent's current contact details here. **No designated agent contact details are published in this draft.** Until that step is completed, do not represent that Connect has completed DMCA agent registration or that this policy alone establishes safe-harbor eligibility.

## Legal note

This policy is a product-policy draft, not legal advice. Have qualified U.S. intellectual-property counsel review it, the counter-notice workflow, record-retention process, and agent registration before launch.
